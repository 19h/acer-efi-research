Interpretation:
  DECODER_EBH changes DRAM bank hashing and is the closest documented
  analogue to the AMD BankSwizzleMode primitive. Intel's 13th-gen
  datasheet classifies MCHBAR D800-D810 as BIOS RW, SMM R, OS R.
  This probe establishes configuration only; it does not establish
  that an OS write is accepted, nor that useful aliases would result.
